## Background & Purpose

This repository contains exploratory data analysis and visualization work carried out as part of my ongoing practice with coding and statistics. I occasionally analyze and visualize real‑world data on topics I am interested in, in order to strengthen my statistical reasoning and maintain hands‑on experience with quantitative methods.

The focus of this work is not only on implementation, but also on interpretation, clarity, and reproducibility, with an emphasis on understanding patterns in human behavior and social data.
